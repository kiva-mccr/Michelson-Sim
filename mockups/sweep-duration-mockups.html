MS-14: how long one sweep takes

Three ways to let you set the sweep time, each drawn inside a cut-down Michelson panel. Press Sweep to watch it run, click the dashed numbers to type, and use "Switch range" to see what each option does when Auto-fit changes the range.

Today one pass (−range to +range) always takes 8 s. I recommend A: it keeps today's behaviour when the range changes, and the panel shows the mirror speed it implies. All three would also accept the scroll wheel on the number (each notch ×1.25 or ÷1.25), like the rail.
