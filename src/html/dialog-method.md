# dialog-method.html

The Method and verification dialog: the method in five sections and the check table (`verBtn`, `verAt`, `ver`). Placed at `<!--@INCLUDE dialog-method.html@-->`.

## Rules and pitfalls

- Every rendered quantity has a row in the check table (filled by `verify.js`), and each method formula cites its source.
- No equation may need sideways scrolling (`tests/layout.py`).
