Method and verification

Every number and pixel on this page comes from the equations below. Nothing is tuned by eye. Run checks recomputes each displayed quantity a second, independent way, using direct numerical integration, for your current spectrum and settings, and reports the largest difference.

1 · Source and coherence

$$\begin{aligned}S(\sigma)&=\sum_{\rm lines}I_n\delta_D(\sigma-\sigma_n)\\&\quad+\sum_{\rm Gauss}a_n\,e^{-(\sigma-\sigma_n)^2/w_n^2}\end{aligned}$$
$$I_n=\sqrt\pi\,a_n w_n\ \text{(Gaussian)},\quad I_n=\sqrt\pi\,a_n w_0\ \text{(line)}$$
$$w_n=\frac{\Delta\sigma_n}{2\sqrt{\ln2}}$$
$$\Delta\sigma_n\equiv\frac{\Delta\lambda_n}{\lambda_n^2}$$
$$\gamma(\delta)=\frac{1}{I_{\rm tot}}\sum_n I_n\,e^{-(\pi w_n\delta)^2}e^{i2\pi\sigma_n\delta}$$
$$I(\delta)=\tfrac{I_{\rm tot}}{2}\left[1+\operatorname{Re}\gamma(\delta)\right]$$
$$S_{\rm out}(\sigma;\delta_0)=S(\sigma)\,\tfrac12\left[1+\cos 2\pi\sigma\delta_0\right]$$
$$l_c^{\rm HWHM}:\ |\gamma(l_c)|=\tfrac12$$
$$l_c^{\rm Mandel}=\int|\gamma|^2d\delta$$

2 · Ring geometry

$$\tan\theta=\frac{r}{r_{\max}}\tan\theta_{\max}$$
$$\delta(u,v)=2d\cos\theta+2a(\alpha_x u+\alpha_y v)$$
$$a=L\tan\theta_{\max},\qquad L=\frac{5\ \text{mm}}{\tan 10^\circ}=28.4\ \text{mm}$$

The screen coordinates $(u,v)$ run from −1 to 1 across the field, so $r=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}$ and $r_{\max}=1$. The lens maps viewing angle to screen radius as $f\tan\theta$. The same $(u,v)$ also gives the mirror-plane position for the tilt wedge, $x=au$, $y=av$.[1][14] This combined picture is the model's main physical assumption.

3 · Pixel averaging (no aliasing)

$$\big\langle e^{-a\delta^2+ib\delta}\big\rangle_{\Delta\sim\mathcal N(0,s^2I)}=\frac{e^{X}}{\sqrt{\det(I-s^2B)}}$$
$$X=-a\bar\delta^2+ib\bar\delta+\tfrac12\kappa^2s^2\,g^{\mathsf T}(I-s^2B)^{-1}g$$
$$\kappa=-2a\bar\delta+ib$$
$$B=\kappa H-2a\,gg^{\mathsf T}$$

Each pixel is a Gaussian footprint with a standard deviation of $s=0.5$ px (a fringe at the pixel pitch keeps $e^{-2\pi^2s^2}<1\%$ of its contrast, so it can't alias into moiré); a moving preview uses the same footprint scaled to its own, larger pixels. Across it the OPD is expanded to second order, $\delta=\bar\delta+g^{\mathsf T}\Delta+\tfrac12\Delta^{\mathsf T}H\Delta$, with the gradient $g$ and the Hessian $H$ computed analytically. The sub-band envelope is expanded to first order. Here $a=\pi^2w^2$ and $b=2\pi\sigma$. The result is a closed-form Gaussian integral; for lines ($a=0$) it is exact for any quadratic OPD. With $H=0$ it reduces to $q^{-1/2}e^{-(a\bar\delta^2+b^2\tau^2/2)/q}\cos(b\bar\delta/q)$, where $q=1+2a\tau^2$ and $\tau=s|g|$. That first-order form is used where the curvature phase $b\,s^2|H|<3\times10^{-4}$, and also for the interferogram's dense-mode colours.

4 · Colour

$$\begin{aligned}(X,Y,Z)&=\sum_k p_k\,\tfrac12\big[1+\langle e^{-a_k\delta^2}\cos b_k\delta\rangle\big]\\&\qquad\times\big(\bar x,\bar y,\bar z\big)(\lambda_k)\end{aligned}$$

Each Gaussian is split into $K$ sub-Gaussians spanning $\pm2.8w$, spaced $\Delta x\,w$ apart with $\Delta x=5.6/(K-1)$, each with width $w_s=\Delta x\,w$. $K$ is at least 15 and large enough that $w_s\le0.012\ \mu\text{m}^{-1}$, with a maximum of 241. The whole Gaussian is always computed, including any part outside the plotted 380–800 nm window: that light carries power (it counts in $I_{\rm tot}$, the interferogram and the ring's brightness profile) but outside 390–780 nm it adds no colour. Each sub-band's colour is its own shape integrated against the colour-matching functions. Their weights are $\propto e^{-(x_k/w_c)^2}$ with $w_c=\sqrt{1-\Delta x^2}\,w$, chosen so the convolution reproduces the parent Gaussian. The colour-matching functions $\bar x\bar y\bar z$ are CVRL CIE 2006 2°.[13] XYZ is converted to linear sRGB with the IEC 61966-2-1 matrix. Out-of-gamut colours are desaturated by $c\to c-\min(c,0)$. Each ring image is scaled so its brightest possible state (fully constructive) maps to 1, then sRGB-encoded.

5 · Definitions, not physics

Both auto ranges start from the OPD $\delta_s$ where $\sum_g I_g e^{-(\pi w_g\delta_s)^2}=0.02\,I_{\rm tot}$. The interferogram spans $\pm1.08\,\delta_s$, rounded up to two significant figures. The mirror rail spans $\pm0.6\,\delta_s$ in $d$, i.e. $\pm1.2\,\delta_s$ in OPD, rounded up to a 1–2–5 value. With no Gaussians they cover one beat period, or 30 fringes for a single line. In the profile and the dense interferogram, the shaded band is $\tfrac12(1\pm|\gamma|)$ at the column centre.
